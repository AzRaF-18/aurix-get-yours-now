Project Poster Showcase

Visual display of the promotional campaign poster created for the AURIX Wireless Headset.

Poster Highlights

  • Primary Headline: "Hear Beyond Silence"
  • Target Audience: Audiophiles, students, and professionals seeking high-fidelity audio.
  • Visual Style: High-contrast dark theme with vibrant neon highlights to reflect premium quality.

Branding Rationale

The Name

Derived from "Auris" (Latin for ear) and "Aura" (an intangible quality or atmosphere). AURIX signifies immersive sound clarity that surrounds the listener.

Brand Identity

Focuses on minimalist luxury and sleek ergonomic design. Designed to project power, precision, and contemporary acoustic engineering.

Core Message

Audio is not just background noise; it is an active experience that enhances focus, emotion, and performance.

English Copywriting & Language Analysis

Element Text Used Persuasive Technique & Purpose
Slogan "Hear Beyond Silence." Paradox: Captures attention by juxtaposing hearing with silence, emphasizing active noise cancellation.
Body Copy "Precision-tuned drivers engineered to isolate every layer of your soundscape." Sensory & Technical Diction: Words like precision-tuned and soundscape create an impression of premium engineering.
Call to Action (CTA) "Experience True Immersion." Imperative Command: Directly prompts the audience to act by promising an elevated experience.
